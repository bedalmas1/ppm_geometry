# Phase 1 — Literature & Repository Audit

Produced per [`PLAN.md`](../PLAN.md) Phase 1 / spec §4, §25 Phase 1. Three parallel research passes covered: (1) next-event models, (2) full-suffix models, (3) outcome-prediction and self-supervised/representation-learning models, plus a check on the ICPM 2027 / ML4PM CFP. Findings below are as retrieved on 2026-08-14 via web search/fetch — repo activity dates, star counts, etc. should be re-verified at the point of actually cloning a repo in Phase 2/3, not trusted indefinitely from this snapshot.

**Note on reproducibility-status entries below:** "verified" means the source (README, `setup.py`, actual code file) was read; it does **not** mean the model was cloned and trained end-to-end. That confirmation is a Phase 3 task.

**Hands-on follow-up (2026-08-14):** the two Family A picks (ProcessTransformer, SuTraN) were actually `git clone`d and their source read directly (not just README/search-snippet review) to pin down license text and the exact z_t extraction point before committing to the roster below. Findings are folded into sections A/B and the roster section. Clones were scratch-only, not added to this repo.

**Decision (revised 2026-08-14): the Family A next-event category uses *two* models, not one.** A single-model next-event category was judged too thin to withstand ICPM/ML4PM reviewer scrutiny, particularly since ProcessTransformer's own venue provenance is arXiv-only. **ProcessTransformer** remains the primary pick (strongest available combination of permissive license, verified installability, and an unambiguous pre-head representation — its arXiv-only provenance is a known, disclosed weakness, not a hidden one). **Camargo et al.'s GenerativeLSTM** (BPM 2019, LNCS 11675, peer-reviewed, Apache-2.0, actively maintained through Oct 2025) is promoted from "optional LSTM baseline" to the second Family A next-event model. This is not padding: pairing a Transformer (ProcessTransformer) with an LSTM (GenerativeLSTM) as the two next-event models gives the ecological/SOTA comparison a genuine architectural contrast within the same predictive objective, which is scientifically meaningful for RQ1 (does architecture alone produce different trajectory geometry even holding the objective fixed?) and directly strengthens, rather than dilutes, the paper's central claim. Its exact prefix-representation extraction point (which LSTM hidden state, pre- vs. post- any shared embedding layers) still needs to be pinned down by reading `AdaptiveBProcess/GenerativeLSTM`'s source in Phase 2/3, the same way ProcessTransformer's and SuTraN's were pinned down here.

**Decision (finalized 2026-08-14, after a dedicated follow-up search): SuTraN + CRTP-LSTM are the two Family A full-suffix models.**

The original pass left CRTP-LSTM's provenance as "not independently verified" — a legitimate weakness given the user's requirement that a second suffix model be defensible to reviewers. A targeted follow-up search resolved this rather than working around it: **CRTP-LSTM is Gunnarsson, vanden Broucke & De Weerdt, "A Direct Data Aware LSTM Neural Network Architecture for Complete Remaining Trace and Runtime Prediction," IEEE Transactions on Services Computing, 16(4), pp. 2330–2342, 2023** — a genuinely peer-reviewed paper in an established IEEE journal, not a workshop note or an unpublished reimplementation. It predicts the full remaining trace + runtime directly (non-autoregressively) via a data-aware LSTM, a real architectural contrast to SuTraN's autoregressive Transformer encoder-decoder. Combined with SuTraN's own ICPM 2024 peer review, **both full-suffix models are independently peer-reviewed**, which was the actual bar the user set — this alone resolves the original concern without adding a third model.

The same follow-up search surfaced one additional candidate, **I3SP** (Xiaomeng-He et al., BPM 2025 Workshops, LNBIP vol. 569, Springer, published 2026-05-03; MIT-licensed; `Xiaomeng-He/I3SP`), an inter-case-aware Seq2Seq LSTM suffix predictor from an author group independent of SuTraN/CRTP-LSTM's KU Leuven lab. It was considered explicitly as a way to defuse a subtler "same-lab, same-codebase" critique (SuTraN and CRTP-LSTM are both from the De Weerdt group and live in the same GitHub repo) — but the user opted to keep the roster at SuTraN + CRTP-LSTM only, judging the now-confirmed independent peer review of both as sufficient justification without the added integration cost (I3SP has no unified training entrypoint and its z_t extraction point was not yet read from source) and without exceeding the spec's stated 3–6 model target. **I3SP is logged here as a deferred candidate**, not rejected outright — worth revisiting only if a reviewer specifically raises the same-lab concern during review.

**Decision (revised 2026-08-14): outcome prediction is dropped from the initial study entirely, not just from Family A.** The verified outcome-prediction ecosystem is dominated by classical ML with no meaningful continuous z_t, and the one deep-learning candidate carries unconfirmed licensing and stale (Colab-era) tooling — so no Family A outcome model is adopted. Rather than fill the gap with a Family B controlled-Transformer-outcome model as originally planned, the user elected to set outcome prediction aside for now entirely, to keep the roster tight and both experiment families focused on the next-event vs. full-suffix objective contrast that is central to RQ1/H1/H2. **H3 (outcome supervision produces strong terminal separation but discards intermediate dynamics) is therefore explicitly deferred and untested in this study**, to be revisited as future work rather than forced with a weak or improvised outcome baseline.

## Final model roster (finalized 2026-08-14, after user-directed revision)

Revised after user review of the initial Phase 1 pass: a single model per Family A category was judged too thin for ICPM/ML4PM reviewers. Final shape: 2 next-event + 2 full-suffix (Family A, each pair independently peer-reviewed and architecturally distinct) + 1 next-event + 1 full-suffix (Family B, controlled comparison) = **6 configurations**, matching the spec's 3–6 target. No outcome objective — dropped entirely (see Section C), H3 explicitly deferred/untested.
